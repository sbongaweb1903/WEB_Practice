Who We Are

AAZ SOLUTIONS is a trusted, multi-service company based in Pietermaritzburg, dedicated to supporting both Public and Private Sector clients with excellence. We specialize in Supply and Delivery, Construction, Cleaning, and Security Services — all under one roof.

With a hands-on approach and a commitment to quality, AAZ SOLUTIONS was founded to make essential services simple, reliable, and professional.

Led by Director Ayabonga Aphile Zikalala, we pride ourselves on being responsive, dependable, and "the best in the game" when it comes to getting the job done right, on time, every time.
